# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR analytics project designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and key HR performance metrics using SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau. This project transforms raw employee data into actionable insights that support strategic workforce planning and employee retention.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The HR Analytics Dashboard is built to explore employee data and uncover insights related to attrition, workforce distribution, employee satisfaction, education background, and departmental performance.

The dashboard helps HR teams, managers, and business leaders make data-driven decisions to improve employee engagement, reduce turnover, and optimize workforce management.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The project was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Interactive dashboards and visualization

📈 Tableau – Advanced data visualization and reporting

🐍 Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and analysis

🗄️ SQL – Data extraction, joins, filtering, and KPI analysis

📂 Excel – Data preparation and initial exploration

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and dashboard measures

📁 File Formats – .xlsx, .sql, .ipynb, .pbix, .twb

---

## 4. Data Source

Source: HR Employee Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Employee demographics
* Department information
* Education background
* Job role details
* Salary information
* Employee attrition status
* Job satisfaction ratings
* Years at company

This dataset is used to understand employee behavior, workforce trends, and attrition patterns.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations. High turnover increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and impacts business performance.

Key questions include:

* Which employees are most likely to leave?
* Which departments have the highest attrition?
* How does job satisfaction affect employee retention?
* What factors contribute to workforce turnover?
* How do age and experience influence attrition?

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To build an interactive HR analytics solution that:

* Monitors employee attrition trends
* Identifies high-risk employee groups
* Tracks workforce demographics
* Analyzes employee satisfaction levels
* Supports data-driven HR decision-making

---

### • Key Visual Insights

📌 KPI Overview

* Total Employees
* Active Employees
* Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate (%)
* Average Age
* Average Salary
* Average Years at Company

📊 Attrition Analysis

Shows employee attrition by:

* Department
* Job Role
* Gender
* Education Field

📈 Employee Demographics

Analyzes:

* Age Distribution
* Gender Distribution
* Marital Status
* Education Levels

📊 Job Satisfaction Analysis

Evaluates satisfaction levels across:

* Departments
* Job Roles
* Employee Groups

📉 Salary & Experience Analysis

Examines relationships between:

* Salary and Attrition
* Experience and Retention
* Tenure and Employee Turnover

🏢 Department Performance Analysis

Compares workforce metrics across departments.

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

📉 Helps reduce employee attrition

📊 Improves workforce planning

🧠 Supports strategic HR decision-making

💰 Reduces recruitment and replacement costs

🎯 Identifies high-risk employee segments

📈 Enhances employee retention strategies
